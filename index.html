📖 Staff Guide
Follow-Up Tracker — How It Works
When a Patient Leaves Without Scheduling
1
Tap the red banner at the top of the screen before they walk out the door.
Takes about 10 seconds.
2
Enter the child's name and phone number. Then answer one quick question: did they leave without scheduling a recare (cleaning) or treatment?
This routes them to the right follow-up list automatically.
3
Select the reason they didn't book if you know it — cost, scheduling conflict, wants to think about it, etc. This helps the team follow up with the right message.
4
Hit Save. The patient appears on their list immediately and follow-up dates are set automatically — weekends and holidays are skipped.
💡 Recare patients get a 2-step follow-up (day-after + 1 week). Treatment patients get a 3-step follow-up (1 day, 3 days, 2 weeks). The right text message is queued automatically for each.
💡 If you miss someone at checkout, use the End-of-Day button to batch-add anyone from that day.
Making Follow-Up Calls
1
Check the Overdue and Due Today counts at the top — those are your priority patients for the day. Both treatment and recare patients are included.
2
Call the patient first. If they answer, log the outcome directly on their card.
3
No answer? Hit 🔇 No Answer — this checks off the step automatically. Then tap 📱 Copy Text, switch to Mango, and paste the message to send a follow-up text from Aliyah. The text will automatically reference whether it's a cleaning or treatment appointment.
4
Left a voicemail? Hit 📞 Left Voicemail — step is marked done and the next follow-up is queued.
💡 Use 🖨 Call Sheet to print today's list if you prefer working from paper.
Closing Out a Patient
1
Patient scheduled? Hit 🎉 Scheduled! — they move off the active list immediately. Always use this button rather than deleting them so our monthly numbers stay accurate.
2
Patient called in on their own to schedule? Use the 📞 Patient Scheduling? button in the header — search their name and mark them scheduled in one tap.
3
Patient declined or went elsewhere? Log the appropriate outcome — they'll be removed from the active list and included in the monthly deactivation report for the office manager.
💡 Only use the ✕ Remove button if a patient was added by mistake.
Daily Denticon Check — End of Day
1
Pull the report from Denticon. In Denticon, go to:
Reports → Appointment Reports → Smart Assist Report Card → Print/Preview
Then download the PDF to your computer.
2
Upload it here. Click the 📄 Denticon Report button in the header, then drag and drop the PDF into the upload area — or click to browse for it.
3
Review the results. The tracker will automatically compare the report against the active patient list. Anyone marked as unscheduled who isn't already on the tracker will appear under Not Logged.
4
Add any missing patients by tapping + Add next to their name and selecting whether it's a Treatment or Recare patient. They'll be added to the correct list immediately.
💡 This is your safety net — even if someone was missed at checkout, this check ensures no patient falls through the cracks.
Monthly Tasks (1st of the Month)
1
A reminder will automatically pop up on the 1st of each month with a list of patients who went elsewhere last month.
2
Print the list and give it to the office manager so they can deactivate these patients in Denticon.
🧊 Gone Cold — Final Re-Engagement
1
When a patient completes all follow-up steps with no response, they automatically move to the 🧊 Gone Cold tab. This applies to both treatment and recare patients.
2
Check the Gone Cold tab regularly. Each patient has a 📱 Copy Final Text button — send this one last re-engagement message to give them a final chance to schedule. The text wording is tailored for treatment vs. recare.
3
On Day 29 the card turns amber with a warning: "Last chance — this patient closes tomorrow." The button changes to Send Final Text Now. Make sure to send it before the patient auto-closes the next day.
4
On Day 30 the patient auto-closes. If they come back to schedule later, use the 📞 Patient Scheduling? button in the header to find and mark them as scheduled.
💡 The morning prompt and your daily browser reminder will include a count of any patients closing tomorrow so you're never caught off guard.
